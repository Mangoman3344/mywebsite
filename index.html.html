Studio philosophy

A house is a set of rooms. A home is a set of habits we design around.

We start every project with a walk-through of how you actually live — where shoes land, who cooks, what gets left on the counter — before we draw a single elevation.

The result is an interior that looks considered because it is: layout first, materials second, decoration last.

How a project runs

From first visit to final snag list.

01

Brief & site visit

We spend half a day in your home before anything is drawn, understanding light, storage and daily routes through the house.

02

Concept & materials

A layout, mood direction and first material palette, tested against your budget before any detailed design begins.

03

Detailed design

Full joinery drawings, lighting plans and specifications, coordinated directly with your architect and contractor.

04

Build & install

Weekly site visits through the build, ending with styling day and a final snag walk-through before handover.
